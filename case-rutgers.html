Web work · Case study · Freelance

A video platform for early childhood educators.

For Rutgers University, I led the creation of a video website for the professional development of early childhood educators, from first research through launch and two years of upkeep.

Client
Rutgers University
Role
Web development & design, freelance
When
Mar 2015 – Mar 2017
Where
New Brunswick, NJ
Status
No longer online

How it came together

I ran the project end to end. The steps are in the order the work happened.

  1. 01

    Research

    Talked with early childhood educators to learn what they needed from professional development videos and how they would use the site.

  2. 02

    Planning

    Wrote a project plan covering scope, timeline and resources.

  3. 03

    UX/UI design

    Designed a clear, easy-to-navigate interface, from wireframes to final screens.

  4. 04

    Content

    Produced and curated video content for educators' professional development.

  5. 05

    Development

    Built the site with modern web technologies, fully responsive across phones, tablets and desktops.

  6. 06

    Testing

    Tested thoroughly and fixed issues before launch.

  7. 07

    Launch

    Launched the site and made it available to educators.

  8. 08

    Maintenance

    Kept the site up to date and running after launch.

Information architecture

Before any design, I mapped the site. Educators reach content by role: coaches, teachers and administrators each get the same three paths to workshops, coaching and professional learning communities.

  • Home
  • Educators
    • Coaches
    • Teachers
    • Administrators

  • About Us
  • Contact Us

What shipped

  • Feature

    Administrators manage content and user access on their own.

  • Ongoing

    Maintenance
