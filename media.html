Graduate School Insider

A podcast for anyone weighing a PhD or MD/PhD in the biomedical sciences. Host Keith Micoli, PhD, assistant dean for Postdoctoral Affairs at NYU School of Medicine, talks with students, postdocs and faculty about what a PhD can lead to, what admissions officers look for, choosing rotation labs and a thesis advisor, and life as a graduate student in New York City. Episodes run 10 to 15 minutes.

I handled recording, publishing and web distribution for the series. It's no longer active. Here are five selected episodes.

  1. 2018

    PhD Admissions: What Do We Look For?

    Applying to graduate programs can feel daunting. Dr. Naoko Tanese and Susanne talk about what sets a PhD application apart from the rest.

    Audio file not added yet: audio/grad-insider-01.mp3

  2. 2018

    What Can I Be with a PhD?

    For college graduates drawn to the sciences, a PhD can seem like a long road. Christine and Ada talk about the doors a PhD can open.

    Audio file not added yet: audio/grad-insider-02.mp3

  3. 2018

    SURP: What Are They and How Are They Useful?

    Many college students get a taste of graduate school by spending a summer in a research program. Dr. Naoko Tanese, Associate Dean for Biomedical Sciences and Director of the Sackler Institute, explains how summer programs help.

    Audio file not added yet: audio/grad-insider-surp.mp3

  4. 2018

    Rotation and Thesis Advisor Selection

    How do you choose labs for rotations, and make sure the lab you end up in is the right one? Graduate students Ashira Lubkin and Emily Radke of the Sackler Institute of Graduate Biomedical Sciences talk it through.

    Audio file not added yet: audio/grad-insider-rotation.mp3

  5. 2018

    Being a Graduate Student in NYC

    Where to live, what it costs and how to get around. Graduate students Lydia Grmai and Phillip Geter on doing a PhD in New York City.
